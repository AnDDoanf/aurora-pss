### Engines

Engines use a rechargeable evasion pool. Engine Room capacity is 25 and Ship Thrusters capacity is 15 across levels; ENG-supported crew improve regeneration, while missile tracking reduces evasion.

##### Expansion Engines {#extension-engines}

![PSS guide illustration 112](/guide-images/en/engines.png)

Fusion Drive Engine (FDE, 3×2) – equivalent to a level 6 engine, can only be obtained through a late support package purchased on the website.
