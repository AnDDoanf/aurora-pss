### Super Weapons

Faction superweapons use mutually exclusive size sets: smaller sets permit more rooms at lower individual strength, while the original rooms retain the large-set limit.

##### Faction superweapons

The Galaxy expansion added one large superweapon for each faction:

- **Federation Superlaser:** a direct energy weapon.
- **Pirate Nuclear Launcher:** launches a heavy nuclear payload.
- **Qtarian Superweapon:** launches the Qtarian faction projectile.

![Federation Superlaser firing](/guide-images/en/super-weapons.gif)

![Pirate Nuclear Launcher firing](/guide-images/en/super-weapons-2.gif)

Small and medium variants were added later. Superweapons now belong to mutually exclusive size sets: smaller sets permit more rooms at lower individual strength, while the original rooms retain the large-set limit.
