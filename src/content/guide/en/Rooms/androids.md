### Androids

Android rooms manufacture droids for repair, defense, healing, assassination, or sacrificial attacks. Queue and ammunition-style production rules should be included in their AI.

##### Expanded droid room {#expanded-droid-room}

![PSS guide illustration 90](/guide-images/en/androids.png)

Visiri Mechbay (VM, 2×2) – a multitasking Android room, equivalent to AS level 6 but uses less power.

![PSS guide illustration 91](/guide-images/en/androids-2.png)

Zongzi Factory (ZF, 2×2) – only produces Zongzi Droid (hybrid between defense and repair droid)

![PSS guide illustration 92](/guide-images/en/androids-3.png)

Ghostly Factory (GF, 2×2) – only produces Ghostly Holo Droids (defense droids)

##### Current Android Studio droids

Hull 14 added **Repair**, **Assassin**, and **Kamikaze** droids. The former Service Droid is now named **Medical Droid**.
