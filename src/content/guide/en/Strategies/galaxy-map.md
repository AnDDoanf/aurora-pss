### Galaxy Map {#galaxy-map}

![PSS guide illustration 138](/guide-images/en/galaxy-map.png)

#### Galaxy progression and encounters

The Galaxy release established the explorable map and Hull 12 progression. The map later became an active PvE and fleet layer rather than only a route between fixed missions.

**Spice Trade** encounters have three difficulty bands and include normal battles, bosses, Stardust Comets, Treasure Fleets, traveling merchants, and special challenges. Moving encounters must be intercepted in their current or next system; an encounter outside the explored map is unavailable until it returns.

![Spice Trade route](/guide-images/en/galaxy-map-2.png)

Spice Traders exchange Void Particles and Stardust for items and partially restock while traveling.

#### Galactic Council and Fleet Wars

Eligible Division A players can vote in the Galactic Council on modifiers for Tournament Finals.

![Galactic Council](/guide-images/en/galaxy-map-3.png)

Fleet Wars lets fleets conquer and manage star systems. Engagements consume Supplies and apply participation, membership-age, and fleet-size requirements. Failed underpowered attacks can trigger cooldowns and higher follow-up Supply costs; successful invasions can reduce Infrastructure.

Fleet Starbases support Supplies, infrastructure activity, expanded hulls, encounters, and ship-room variants. The Debris Harvester produces limited random raw materials for fleet members and consumes Supplies to recharge. Starbases also gained Reisen Drone Launcher and room-based or area Starbase Cruise Missile damage. Hull 14 added updated missions and further Galaxy Map encounters.
